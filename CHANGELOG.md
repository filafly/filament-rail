## v1.0.1 - 2026-11-04

- No code changes.

## v1.0.0 - 2026-11-04

First public release.

- A slim icon rail beside the sidebar: a logo tile, a tile for each of the user's tenants (with optional status dots and per-tenant tint), tenant menu links, your own pinned links and the database notifications bell.
- On desktop the rail stands in for Filament's tenant menu and notifications bell, in the sidebar or the topbar. Below the `lg` breakpoint it hides and Filament's own controls come back.
- Supports Filament v4 and v5.
